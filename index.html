Founder

Simon Thomas

Simon has spent close to two decades shaping performance systems across international and professional rugby, Major League Baseball and international consulting. His career runs from hands-on programme builds at the Tasman Mako and Canterbury, through eight years with the Crusaders, the final four leading the performance department across four consecutive Super Rugby titles, to department leadership at the Western Force and the Boston Red Sox.

Alongside embedded roles he has advised Brazil Rugby on a full national framework build, supported Singapore Rugby through a two-year remote engagement, provided leadership oversight at the Panasonic Wild Knights in Japan and serves as High Performance Advisor to the Major League Rugby Players Association.
