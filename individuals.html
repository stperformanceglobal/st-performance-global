Who This Is For

Three people we help most often

The Practitioner Becoming a Leader

You were promoted because you were the best technician in the room, and nobody taught you the next job. Simon made this transition himself, from hands-on delivery at the Tasman Mako through to pure leadership at the Boston Red Sox, and has mentored others through it across rugby, Australian football and baseball. The work covers delegation, feedback, role clarity and the balcony discipline of leading rather than operating.

The Established Head Wanting an Outside Eye

You lead a department and have nobody to think out loud with. Structured mentoring gives you a sounding board who has led championship departments, built one from zero and advised national programmes, with session preparation, priority tracking and reflection built into the process.

The Executive or Professional Under Pressure

The tools of elite sport translate. Coaching draws on the same anchors Simon uses with professional teams: the growth equation of stress plus rest, psychological safety, the Job Demands-Resources model and his own Shadow Load framework for monitoring the load you carry while looking after everyone else.

Every membership starts with baseline assessment and profiling, so the support is designed around where you actually are. Simon has made every transition he coaches others through, which is why the work starts with your context.
What Actually Happens

Your first thirty days

01Day One

A welcome email with your assessment

No waiting and no scheduling required to begin. The assessment link arrives immediately and you complete it in your own time.

02Week One

Your baseline, returned

The assessment produces a profile of where you actually are. Everything that follows is shaped by that profile. Everything after this point is shaped by it.

03Ongoing

Guided support that evolves with you

Structured support arrives without you needing to book anything. It adapts as your assessments and progress change, so the guidance moves as you do. Calls are there when you want them, though the value does not depend on booking one.

04Professional Tier

Your monthly session

A 60 minute video call with follow up action points, held every month. If you want more contact in a given month, additional sessions are available at member rate, which is 25% below the one-off price.

Cancel or change tier at any time. If you are not sure which level fits, a discovery call costs nothing and carries no obligation.
